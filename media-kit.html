Long bio

Prateek Saxena is a business operator based in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates. He serves as Group General Manager of Al Zaabi Group, a diversified UAE business group with interests across multiple real-economy sectors, including healthcare, automotive, real estate, building materials, contracting, manufacturing, trading, retail, e-commerce, food and beverage and education.

Prateek's work is focused on the operating layer of business: how companies actually run, scale and improve. That includes strategy execution, financial discipline, management reporting, leadership systems, digital transformation and practical AI adoption.

He began his career close to the operating floor, with early roles at John Deere and Bose Corporation in the United States. He later worked in corporate strategy at Delma Emirates Group before joining Al Zaabi Group in Abu Dhabi, where his responsibilities grew across operations, strategy and group management roles.

Prateek studied Industrial Engineering at Purdue University and completed executive education at INSEAD, including work on business transformation and AI. His point of view combines engineering discipline, financial rigour and practical management experience across operating businesses. He writes on prateeksaxena.me about business operations, leadership, the UAE real economy, enterprise AI adoption, harness engineering for AI agents and the management systems needed to turn ambition into execution.
