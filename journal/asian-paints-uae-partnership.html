A strategic partnership with Asian Paints

Representing one of the world's largest paint manufacturers across the UAE.

By Prateek Saxena · March 2024 · Abu Dhabi, UAE

Who represents Asian Paints in the UAE? Al Zaabi Group represents Asian Paints, one of the world's largest paint manufacturers, across the UAE under a strategic partnership announced in March 2024.

In March 2024, Al Zaabi Group entered a strategic partnership with Asian Paints, one of the world's largest paint manufacturers, to represent the brand across the UAE. The group hosted Amit Syngle, MD and CEO of Asian Paints, as the partnership was announced.

Frequently asked questions

What is Asian Paints Berger?

Asian Paints Berger is the Middle East arm of Asian Paints, one of the world's largest paint manufacturers.
