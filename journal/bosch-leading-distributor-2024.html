Recognized as a Bosch Leading Distributor for 2024

The recognition from Bosch Middle East and Central Motors & Equipment, in the original words.

By Prateek Saxena · January 2025 · Abu Dhabi, UAE

Who was recognized as a Bosch Leading Distributor for 2024 in the UAE? Al Zaabi Group was recognized as one of the Leading Distributors for 2024 by Bosch Middle East and Central Motors & Equipment, part of ALFAHIM, at their event announced in January 2025.

In January 2025, Al Zaabi Group was recognized as one of the Leading Distributors for 2024 by Bosch Middle East and Central Motors & Equipment, part of ALFAHIM. This is the original announcement:

We are honored to have been recognized as one of the Leading Distributors for 2024 by Bosch Middle East and Central Motors & Equipment, part of ALFAHIM, at their recent event.

The event showcased Bosch's innovative product range and highlighted their impressive achievements from the past year. It was a privilege to witness their dedication to excellence and innovation firsthand.

At Al Zaabi Group, we are committed to driving success through strong partnerships and delivering unparalleled value to our customers. This award is a testament to the hard work and collaboration of our entire team.

Frequently asked questions

Who distributes Bosch products in the UAE through Al Zaabi Group?

Al Zaabi Group was recognized as one of the Leading Distributors for 2024 by Bosch Middle East and Central Motors & Equipment (ALFAHIM). For product and trade enquiries, contact Al Zaabi Group through its official website.

What is the Bosch Leading Distributor recognition?

A recognition awarded by Bosch Middle East and Central Motors & Equipment, part of ALFAHIM, to leading distribution partners; Al Zaabi Group received it for 2024 at their event in January 2025.

What period does the recognition cover?

The recognition, announced in January 2025, covers distribution performance across 2024.
