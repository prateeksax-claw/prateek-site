Al Zaabi Group and Saint-Gobain, across the years

Recognitions, a knowledge-space visit and a 360th birthday: the long arc of a distribution relationship.

By Prateek Saxena · 2022 to 2025 · Abu Dhabi, UAE

Who is a Saint-Gobain distributor in the UAE? Al Zaabi Group is a long-standing Saint-Gobain distributor in the UAE, named one of Saint-Gobain's Leading Distributors in 2024 and host of its 360-year celebration in Abu Dhabi.

Some partnerships are announcements. Others are relationships that compound over years. Our work with Saint-Gobain, the French building-materials group with more than three centuries of history, is the second kind.

The milestones tell the story. In 2022, Saint-Gobain recognised the work of the Al Zaabi Group team. In 2023, forty of our team members visited AL-MUNTADA, Saint-Gobain's knowledge space, to go deeper on products and application. In November 2024, we were named one of Saint-Gobain's Leading Distributors. And in February 2025, we celebrated 360 years of Saint-Gobain with an event held at one of our branches in Abu Dhabi.

A distribution relationship that lasts is really an operating relationship. It survives on stock discipline, trained counter staff, honest forecasting and the willingness to invest in knowledge, not just inventory.

When a 360-year-old company chooses to celebrate its birthday at your branch, that is not a marketing moment. It is a statement about trust built over time.

Originally shared on LinkedIn.

What a long-term distribution relationship involves

Saint-Gobain's product world spans construction materials where specification, application and after-sales knowledge all matter. A distributor that lasts in that world does more than move boxes. It forecasts honestly, keeps trained people at the counter, invests in product knowledge, and treats the brand's standard as its own.

That is why the AL-MUNTADA knowledge visit matters as much as the awards. Sending forty team members to go deeper on products and application is what turns a distribution agreement into a capability.

The recognitions in context

Being named one of Saint-Gobain's Leading Distributors is a measured recognition. It reflects sustained performance across availability, coverage and commercial results, assessed by the brand itself. Earning it repeatedly, and being chosen to host the 360-year celebration in Abu Dhabi, says the relationship works at the operating level, year after year.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Saint-Gobain's distributor in the UAE?

Al Zaabi Group is a long-standing Saint-Gobain distributor in the UAE, named one of its Leading Distributors in 2024 and host of Saint-Gobain's 360-year celebration in Abu Dhabi in February 2025.

What is Saint-Gobain?

Saint-Gobain is a French multinational building-materials group, founded in 1665, active in construction products worldwide.

Where can I buy Saint-Gobain products in the UAE?

Saint-Gobain construction products are available in the UAE through its distribution network, which includes Al Zaabi Group. For product and trade enquiries, contact Al Zaabi Group through its official website.

How long has Al Zaabi Group worked with Saint-Gobain?

The relationship has run for years: Saint-Gobain recognised the Al Zaabi Group team in 2022, hosted a team knowledge visit at AL-MUNTADA in 2023, named the group a Leading Distributor in November 2024 and celebrated its 360th anniversary at an Al Zaabi branch in Abu Dhabi in February 2025.

For product availability or trade enquiries in the UAE, reach Al Zaabi Group directly.
