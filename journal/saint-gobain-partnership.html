Partnership Story · Saint-Gobain

Al Zaabi Group and Saint-Gobain, across the years

A decade-plus collaboration, told through the original announcements.

By Prateek Saxena · 2022 to 2025 · Abu Dhabi, UAE

Who is a Saint-Gobain distributor in the UAE? Al Zaabi Group is a long-standing Saint-Gobain distributor in the UAE, a collaboration spanning over a decade, and was awarded as one of the Leading Distributors for Saint-Gobain Gyproc at the 4th Saint-Gobain Gypsum Trophy Awards 2024.

The collaboration between Al Zaabi Group and Saint-Gobain GCC spans more than a decade. The milestones below are told in the original words of each announcement.

2022: recognition

Honored to be recognized by Saint Gobain for the hard work of the entire Al Zaabi Group team.

November 2022 · original post

2023: AL-MUNTADA, Masdar City

Recently, Al Zaabi Group had the privilege of visiting Saint Gobain's innovative Al-Muntada office in Masdar City, Abu Dhabi. This visit emphasized our mutual commitment to sustainable construction and forward-thinking innovation.

Our collaboration with Saint Gobain, spanning over a decade, has matured significantly this year, extending to more products than ever before.

This is a direct result of the dedication, commitment, and strength of the entire Al Zaabi Group team. Our team's collective efforts, coupled with a robust partnership, have solidified our roots and prepared us for a future of sustainable growth.

June 2023 · original posts · Saint-Gobain Gulf later welcomed 40 Al Zaabi Group team members to AL-MUNTADA, where Prateek Saxena presented the group's businesses.

2024: Leading Distributor, Gypsum Trophy Awards

It is an incredible honor for Al Zaabi Group to be awarded as one of the Leading Distributor for Saint-Gobain Gyproc at the 4th Saint-Gobain Gypsum Trophy Awards 2024!

This recognition underscores our strong partnership with Saint-Gobain GCC, a global leader in building sustainable solutions. The Gypsum Trophy celebrates innovation, collaboration, and craftsmanship, and we remain committed to setting industry standards and delivering exceptional value to our customers.

November 2024 · original post

2025: 360 years of Saint-Gobain, celebrated in Abu Dhabi

Yesterday, we celebrated 360 years of Saint-Gobain with a successful event held at one our branches in Abu Dhabi with the SG Demo Van, showcasing the innovation of Saint-Gobain Weber France & Gyproc.

The turnout was fantastic. Thank you to our clients, partners, and team members for making it a success. The event featured live product demonstrations and hands on experiences.

This event reinforced the strong partnership between Al Zaabi Group and Saint-Gobain GCC. Our collaboration is built on trust and a commitment to delivering excellence in building material solutions across the UAE.

February 2025 · original post

Al Zaabi Group is a long-standing Saint-Gobain distributor in the UAE. The collaboration with Saint-Gobain GCC spans over a decade and covers products including Saint-Gobain Gyproc and Weber.
