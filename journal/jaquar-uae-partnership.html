A new chapter with Jaquar

The partnership with India's largest bathroom solutions company, in the original words.

By Prateek Saxena · March 2024 · Abu Dhabi, UAE

Who is Jaquar's partner in the UAE? Al Zaabi Group signed an MOU to represent Jaquar, India's largest bathroom solutions company, in the Abu Dhabi region, announced in March 2024, bringing Jaquar's product lineup including the luxury Artize range to its UAE clientele.

In March 2024, Al Zaabi Group announced its partnership with Jaquar & Company Private Limited. This is the original announcement:

With great enthusiasm, we announce a pivotal partnership between Al Zaabi Group and Jaquar, India's largest bathroom solutions company.

Jaquar, celebrated for its impeccable quality and aesthetic excellence, brings to the table a rich legacy dating back to 1960. With a presence in over 55 countries, Jaquar's commitment to excellence is evident in its extensive product range that caters to luxury, premium, and value segments. Their luxury brand, Artize, is particularly noteworthy for those who seek bathroom solutions that are not just functional but are crafted pieces of art.

This partnership embodies our shared values of excellence and sustainability, with Jaquar's commitment to eco-friendly solutions aligning perfectly with our vision for the future. We are excited to bring Jaquar's acclaimed product lineup, including the luxurious Artize range, to our esteemed clientele in UAE.

Prateek Saxena on LinkedIn, March 2024 · original post

The partnership event

In May 2024, the partnership was marked with an event in Abu Dhabi attended by top contractors, consultants and architects. From the original post:

This strategic alliance marks a significant milestone in our journey towards innovative and luxurious bathroom solutions. The event was graced by top contractors, consultants and architects from Abu Dhabi, further emphasizing the importance and impact of this collaboration.

A special thanks to Mr. Alok Gupta, Sr. Vice President & Global Business Head of Jaquar Group, for his informative presentation, which provided valuable insights into the future of bathroom solutions and highlighted the innovations Jaquar is bringing to the industry.

May 2024 · original post

Frequently asked questions

Who is Jaquar's partner in the UAE?

What is Jaquar?

Jaquar is India's largest bathroom solutions company, with a legacy dating back to 1960, presence in over 55 countries, and a product range spanning luxury, premium and value segments, including the luxury brand Artize.

What is Artize?

Artize is Jaquar's luxury brand, highlighted in the partnership announcement as bathroom solutions crafted as pieces of art.
