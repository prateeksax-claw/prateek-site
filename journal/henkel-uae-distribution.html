Distributing Henkel in the UAE: from products to practice

A strategic distributor relationship, and why we put our team through hands-on product training.

By Prateek Saxena · July 2025 · Abu Dhabi, UAE

Who is a Henkel distributor in the UAE? Al Zaabi Group is a strategic distributor for Henkel in the UAE, covering ranges such as Polybit waterproofing, tiling and flooring systems, with trained teams across the Emirates.

Al Zaabi Group is a strategic distributor for Henkel, the German adhesives and construction-chemicals group, in the UAE.

In July 2025, our team went deep on Henkel's Polybit range in a hands-on training session at NH47: waterproofing, tiling and flooring systems, applied and tested rather than just presented. Construction chemicals are technical products, and the difference between a good outcome and a failure on site is usually knowledge, not price.

That is why we invest in training. A distributor that only moves boxes adds margin. A distributor whose people can advise on application adds value, and that is the standard we hold ourselves to.

The Henkel relationship is a good example of distribution as a practice: products, knowledge and service standards moving together.
