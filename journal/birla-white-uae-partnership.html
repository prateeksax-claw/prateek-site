Bringing Birla White to the UAE

Behind the exclusive agreement that brought the white-cement and wall-care brand of Grasim Industries to the UAE.

By Prateek Saxena · July 2025 · Abu Dhabi, UAE

Who distributes Birla White in the UAE? Al Zaabi Group holds an exclusive agreement to distribute Birla White, the white-cement and wall-care brand of Grasim Industries (Aditya Birla Group), in the UAE, announced in July 2025.

In July 2025, Al Zaabi Group entered an exclusive agreement to bring Birla White to the UAE. Birla White is the white-cement and wall-care brand of Grasim Industries, part of the Aditya Birla Group, and one of the most recognised names in its category. We were honoured to host Himanshu Kapania, Managing Director of Grasim Industries and Business Head for Birla White, as the agreement came together.

From the operator's chair, a distribution partnership is not a signature on a page. It is a commitment to coverage, stock, technical training, and a service standard that has to hold across every emirate and every counter where the product is sold. That is the part of the work I care about most: what happens after the announcement.

For the UAE construction and building-materials market, the timing matters. Demand for quality finishing products keeps growing with the country's development pipeline, and white cement and wall-care putty sit at the heart of that finishing work.

This partnership also says something about how we operate. Global brands choose distribution partners on execution: logistics, reach, reporting and reliability. Earning an exclusive mandate is a signal that the operating system behind the business works.
