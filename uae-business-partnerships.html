UAE business partnerships

Six brand relationships, one operating discipline. The stories behind Al Zaabi Group's UAE partnerships.

By Prateek Saxena · Updated 3 July 2026 · Abu Dhabi

Who does Al Zaabi Group partner with in the UAE? Al Zaabi Group's brand partnerships include Saint-Gobain, Bosch Professional, Henkel, Birla White, Asian Paints and Jaquar, spanning building materials, construction chemicals, professional tools, paints and bath solutions across the UAE.

Partnerships are the clearest external evidence of operating discipline. Global brands choose their UAE partners on execution: coverage, availability, trained people, honest reporting and results that repeat. These pages tell each story from the operator's chair.

Building materials and construction

Saint-Gobain: a relationship across years, from Leading Distributor recognitions to hosting the 360-year celebration in Abu Dhabi.
Birla White: an exclusive agreement bringing Grasim Industries' white-cement and wall-care brand to the UAE.
Henkel: strategic distribution of construction chemicals, backed by hands-on Polybit training.
Jaquar: bath and sanitary solutions joining the building-materials portfolio.

Tools and paints

Bosch Professional: named one of Bosch Middle East's Leading Distributors for 2024.
Asian Paints: a strategic partnership representing one of the world's largest paint manufacturers across the UAE.

What operators must get right after signing

The announcement is the easy part. What follows is the work: stock that is actually available, logistics that hold in summer, counter staff who know the product, service that answers, and numbers reported honestly to the brand. Partnerships compound when the operating system behind them is reliable. That is the thread connecting every page above, and it is the same discipline I write about in the Journal.

Frequently asked questions

Which brands does Al Zaabi Group partner with in the UAE?

Al Zaabi Group's partnerships include Saint-Gobain, Bosch Professional, Henkel, Birla White (Grasim Industries, Aditya Birla Group), Asian Paints and Jaquar, alongside other brands across its businesses.

What do these partnerships cover?

They span building materials, construction chemicals, professional tools, paints and bath solutions: distribution, representation and recognitions earned across years of trade in the UAE.

How can I make a trade enquiry?

For product availability and trade enquiries related to any of these brands, contact Al Zaabi Group through its official website, alzaabigroup.com.
