Your information & choices

Privacy

This website is maintained by Prateek Saxena. This notice explains the optional analytics used on prateeksaxena.me and the choices available to you.

Optional analytics

If you choose “Allow analytics,” Google Analytics measures visits, page views and interactions such as clicks on email and LinkedIn links. Microsoft Clarity provides interaction analysis, heatmaps and session recordings to help identify usability problems. These services can process device, browser, page, interaction and network-related information.

These optional scripts are not loaded until you allow analytics. Advertising personalization and Google Signals are disabled in this site’s analytics configuration. The website does not send the contents of an email enquiry through its custom analytics events.

Your choice

Choose “Essential only” to use the website without these optional analytics scripts. Your choice is saved in your browser so it can be remembered on later visits. You can change it below. If you withdraw permission after analytics has loaded, the page reloads to stop further optional tracking.

Contacting me

Email links open your email application. If you choose to send a message, the details you include are used to respond to your enquiry. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is unnecessary for an initial conversation.

Hosting and performance

Cloudflare hosts and protects this website and processes information needed to deliver it. Cloudflare Web Analytics also measures website performance through a separate beacon on the production domain. This measurement remains enabled independently of the Google Analytics and Clarity choice above. Cloudflare states that its performance measurement does not use cookies or local storage, or fingerprint individual visitors. See Cloudflare’s description of the data collected.

Other services and links

Following a link to LinkedIn or another external website takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

For more information, see Google’s privacy policy and Microsoft’s privacy statement.
