About

An operator who makes scale repeatable.

At the helm of Al Zaabi Group, I lead the strategy and operations of 42 companies across 11 sectors — from healthcare and automotive to real estate, manufacturing and e-commerce. My work is turning opportunity into tangible results: establishing and scaling brands across the UAE through collaborative leadership and data-driven decisions.
