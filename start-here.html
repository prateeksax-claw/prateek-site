AI transformation is not about prompts. It is about the operating systems around AI: workflows, permissions, memory, tools, verification, management rhythm and human judgment.

What is prateeksaxena.me? This is where Prateek Saxena, an Abu Dhabi business operator running companies across eleven sectors, writes about putting AI agents to work in real business. The core of the site is the In a Suit essay series, backed by named frameworks and plain-language definitions.

Everything here comes from operating real companies, not from commentary. If you read only one thing, read the essay that started it: Vibe Coding in a Suit. If you have a little more time, pick the path that matches you.

About the In a Suit series

In a Suit is a series about doing modern technical work from the business operator's chair: building tools by briefing AI agents, testing agents against real Monday mornings, and the operating discipline that makes AI reliable inside a company. It grew out of the prologue essay, After the Prompt, and continues roughly weekly in the Journal.

Common questions

Where should I start reading?

Start with Vibe Coding in a Suit, then I Don't Code. I Brief Agents., then The Monday Morning Preflight. The three essays form the In a Suit series and build on each other in that order.

Business operators, executives and transformation leaders who want practical ways to put AI agents to work inside real companies, written by an operator running diversified businesses in the UAE, not a commentator.
