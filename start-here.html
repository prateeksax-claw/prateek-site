About the In a Suit series

In a Suit is a series about doing modern technical work from the business operator's chair: building tools by briefing AI agents, testing agents against real Monday mornings, and the operating discipline that makes AI reliable inside a company. It grew out of the prologue essay, After the Prompt, and continues in the Journal.
