The Landscape

The state of enterprise AI

The headline numbers are real. The gap between them is the whole story — and the reason an operator's perspective matters.

~78%
of organizations report using AI in at least one function
~11%
of enterprises actually run AI agents in production
40%
of enterprise apps expected to embed task-specific agents by end of 2026
~5×
average reported ROI on AI investment that reaches production

Figures compiled from public 2025–2026 industry research (McKinsey State of AI, Gartner, Deloitte, Google Cloud). Directional, not a forecast — and a reminder that adoption claims far outrun production reality.
