What I Do

At the helm of Al Zaabi Group, I lead the strategic direction and operational management of a portfolio of 42 companies across Healthcare, Automotive, Building Materials, E-Commerce, Real Estate, F&B, Education, Transport, and Manufacturing. My approach combines global perspective — shaped by Purdue University and INSEAD — with deep local expertise. I believe in collaborative leadership and data-driven decision-making to create sustainable business value.

How I Think

Three short takes on the ideas that shape how I lead and build.

On Data

Most businesses have more data than they can use. The problem isn't access — it's the habit of looking. I've spent years building that habit into teams.

On Scale

Growing from 10 to 100 people is easy. Growing from 100 to 1,000 without losing speed is the real challenge. It requires systems more than talent.

On the UAE Market

The UAE rewards speed and relationships equally. The businesses that win are the ones that move fast AND build trust. Neither alone is enough.
