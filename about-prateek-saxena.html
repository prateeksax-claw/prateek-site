About Prateek Saxena

Abu Dhabi-based business operator, builder and strategist, working where strategy meets execution across the UAE real economy.

Prateek Saxena is an Abu Dhabi-based business operator, builder and strategist. He serves as Group General Manager of Al Zaabi Group and works across the UAE's real economy, including healthcare, automotive, real estate, manufacturing, trading, e-commerce and education.

His work sits between strategy and execution. The focus is practical: turn business direction into operating rhythm, financial discipline, technology adoption and repeatable scale.

Short bio

Prateek Saxena is a business operator based in Abu Dhabi, UAE. He is Group General Manager of Al Zaabi Group, a diversified UAE business group with interests across multiple real-economy sectors. A Purdue-trained industrial engineer with INSEAD executive education, Prateek writes about business operations, leadership, finance, technology and practical AI adoption in operating companies.

What Prateek works on

Prateek works on the operating layer of business: how companies actually run, scale and improve. His work includes:

  • business strategy and execution
  • operations across diversified business portfolios
  • financial discipline and management reporting
  • digital transformation and enterprise AI adoption
  • leadership systems, governance and accountability
  • practical ways to improve decision making across teams

The recurring question is simple: how does ambition become execution? A strategy only matters if teams can turn it into routines, numbers, decisions and follow-through.

Career path

Prateek began his career close to the operating floor, with early roles at John Deere and Bose Corporation in the United States. Those roles shaped his engineering-led view of work: understand the system, measure what matters, remove friction and improve the process.

He later worked in corporate strategy at Delma Emirates Group before joining Al Zaabi Group in Abu Dhabi. At Al Zaabi Group, his roles have included Operations and Strategy Manager, Group Deputy General Manager and Group General Manager.

That path, from manufacturing and operations into diversified group leadership, shapes the way he writes and works. The point of strategy is not the document. The point is what changes in the business after the document is written.

Education

Prateek studied Industrial Engineering at Purdue University and completed executive education at INSEAD, including a Certificate in Global Management and work on transforming businesses with AI. He is also pursuing the Certified Management Accountant (CMA) credential with the Institute of Management Accountants.

The combination is practical: engineering discipline, financial rigour and a management lens for scaling businesses.

AI and technology

Prateek writes about AI from an operator's point of view, not as a slogan. His current writing looks at how real businesses can use AI safely and usefully. The model is only one part of the system. Businesses also need context, tools, permissions, verification, memory, audit trails and human judgment. He refers to this operating layer as harness engineering: the system around an AI model that lets it act inside real work.

He also writes about Self-Improvement Protocols, or SIPs, for AI agents. A SIP is a disciplined loop for learning from outcomes, correcting mistakes and improving the system without removing human oversight. You can read the full argument in After the Prompt: The Operator's Harness.
