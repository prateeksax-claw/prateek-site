Questions, answered

What does Prateek Saxena write about?

Prateek writes essays on agentic AI, vibe coding in a suit, AI harness engineering and the operating systems behind real businesses, alongside notes on leadership, finance and the UAE real economy. Key terms are defined on the definitions page.

What is the In a Suit series?

In a Suit is Prateek Saxena's essay series about doing modern technical work from the business operator's chair. The series so far: Vibe Coding in a Suit (No. 1), I Don't Code. I Brief Agents. (No. 2) and The Monday Morning Preflight (No. 3). It grew out of the prologue essay, After the Prompt: The Operator's Harness.

What is the Logbook?

The Logbook is the record beside the essays: partnership stories and recognitions with brands such as Saint-Gobain, Bosch, Henkel, Birla White, Asian Paints and Jaquar, plus events and milestones from Al Zaabi Group's businesses across the UAE.
