Questions, answered

What does Prateek Saxena write about?

Prateek writes essays on agentic AI, vibe coding in a suit, AI harness engineering and the operating systems behind real businesses, alongside notes on leadership, finance and the UAE real economy. Key terms are defined on the definitions page.

What is the In a Suit series?

In a Suit is Prateek Saxena's essay series about doing modern technical work from the business operator's chair. No. 1 is Vibe Coding in a Suit and No. 2 is I Don't Code. I Brief Agents.

What do the updates and milestones cover?

Partnership stories and recognitions with brands such as Saint-Gobain, Bosch, Henkel, Birla White, Asian Paints and Jaquar, plus events and milestones from Al Zaabi Group's businesses across the UAE.
